YOUR WEBSITE: HOW TO USE AND EDIT

PUBLISH
1. Upload everything in this folder to the root of your GitHub repo.
2. Repo > Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root) > Save.

THINGS TO FILL IN LATER
- Contact form: sign up at formspree.io, create a form, and replace YOUR_FORM_ID in contact.html.
- LinkedIn: in contact.html, find the LinkedIn comment, paste your profile address, and remove the comment markers.
- My Work (work.html): links for the Media Beyond The Numbers column and the SABC interview are marked with comments. Copy any <li> block to add new work.
- Viewpoint: open articles.js, copy one article block to the top of the list and edit it. The Home page shows the three newest automatically.

FILES
index.html, about.html, work.html, viewpoint.html, contact.html, 404.html
style.css (colours are at the top, in the :root block)
articles.js (Viewpoint articles), main.js (small script, no need to edit)
images/ and assets/ (photos, your resume PDF and the 2025 analytics deck)
