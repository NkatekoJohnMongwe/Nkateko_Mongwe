Reporting and analytics projects

  • Nedbank OpCom report

    Compiled the full monthly Nedbank OpCom report in Excel at Lucid Performance Agency. It gave a detailed, attribution-based view of total paid media value and performance, tracked against AppsFlyer and Adobe Analytics conversion data.

  • Client media reporting dashboards

    At PHD Media SA I followed a structured process for every dashboard: a reporting inventory covering taxonomy, data sources, raw inputs, objectives and recipients. I built them in Looker Studio, with BigQuery and SQL joins behind the automation, and supported them afterwards.

  • Vodacom digital media dashboards

    Built Datorama dashboards for every Vodacom business unit running digital campaigns, along with frameworks that track budget against spend and plan against actual media performance.

  • Viewer snapshot report for VIU

    Developed a report that tracks daily and weekly platform engagement, and packaged global platform data from Tableau into insights for the business.
