About me

I've spent more than 13 years working in data, insights, analytics and reporting across marketing, media and digital.

I'm self-motivated, objective-driven and detail-oriented. I like to understand how a process works from end to end, and I advocate for high-quality outputs. Where others see figures, I look for the meaning and reason behind the statistics.

I'm based in Johannesburg. I also run Lumiq, a consultancy focused on marketing, media and AI intelligence, and I take on freelance data, insights and analytics projects.

What I do

  • Measurement and reporting

    Building and managing marketing and media reporting dashboards, and applying automation and machine learning to anomaly detection and performance optimisation.

  • Media market intelligence

    Using currency data to turn media landscape trends, competitor activity and audience behaviour into planning and placement decisions.

  • Customer insights

    Finding audience segments, behavioural patterns and value opportunities in first-, zero- and third-party data.

  • Tracking and analytics set-up

    Implementing, auditing and optimising web and app tagging across Google Tag Manager, Firebase and Adobe Launch, including server-side tracking.

Why work with me

  • I've seen every side of the industry

    I've worked for media owners such as Primedia, Mediamark, The Citizen and Habari Media, at agency networks including WPP, Omnicom and Publicis, and client-side at Vodacom. I know what each side needs from the data.

  • I turn data into a story people act on

    Clear narratives matter more than more charts. I focus on the insight, the recommendation and the next step.

  • I own reporting end to end

    From taxonomy and data sources to dashboards, scheduled refreshes and troubleshooting, I make sure the numbers stay accurate and the reporting keeps running.

  • I bring a broad toolkit

    Google Analytics 4, Adobe Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Power BI, BigQuery, SQL, Datorama, Supermetrics, Advanced Excel, Telmar, Nielsen Ad Intel, GWI, Euromonitor, Statista, AppsFlyer, Firebase and the Google Marketing Platform.
